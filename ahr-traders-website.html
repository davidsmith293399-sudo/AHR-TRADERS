STRATEGY 01

Price Action

Read Price Before Indicators

Price action focuses on understanding how price moves around important areas instead of depending on indicators alone.

Key areas can include previous highs and lows, support/resistance, breakouts, retests and rejection candles.

STRATEGY 02

Liquidity Trading

Identify Important Liquidity

Traders often monitor areas where orders may accumulate, such as equal highs, equal lows, previous day high and previous day low.

A liquidity sweep followed by a strong reaction can provide useful information about potential market direction.

STRATEGY 03

Sniper Scalping

Multi-Timeframe Sniper Approach

Start with the higher timeframe for context, then move down to confirmation and execution.

Example workflow: M15 context → M5 confirmation → M1 execution.

The goal is to wait for multiple conditions to align instead of entering simply because price reaches a level.

STRATEGY 04

Market Structure

Understand Market Direction

Market structure helps traders identify whether price is forming higher highs and higher lows or lower highs and lower lows.

Break of Structure and Change of Character can be used as additional context when evaluating a potential change in market direction.

STRATEGY 05

Risk Management

Protect Your Trading Capital

A good strategy is not enough without proper risk management.

Position size should be calculated based on account size, stop-loss distance and the amount of capital you are willing to risk.

Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.

STRATEGY 06

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Context → Confirmation → Execution

Higher timeframes can provide market context, while lower timeframes can be used for more precise confirmation and execution.

A possible workflow is: H1/H4 context → M15 structure → M5 confirmation → M1 execution.

LEARNING PROCESS

A Simple Trading Workflow

01

Determine the broader market direction and important price levels.

02

Identify liquidity, support/resistance or another important market area.

03

Define stop loss, position size and target before entering the trade.
